The Method

What KP
Astrology is.

KP Astrology, also known as Krishnamurti Paddhati, is a precise and event-oriented system of astrology focused on timing and practical judgment. It is known for working with houses, nakshatras, sub-lords, and cuspal analysis to understand what is likely to happen and when. It is often preferred by people who want a more structured and specific approach rather than broad general statements.

At Anant Nakshatra, KP Astrology is used as a consultation method, not entertainment. The goal is to help you understand your situation more clearly so you can make better decisions in career, marriage, business, family, and other important areas of life. If remedies are genuinely required, they may be discussed, but they are not the core of the service and nothing extra is pushed.
